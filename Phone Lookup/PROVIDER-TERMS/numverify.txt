NUMVERIFY (APILayer number_verification)

Sends the subject's phone number as digits only (no +) as the `number` query parameter to api.apilayer.com, authenticated with your API key in the `apikey` header (providers/numverify.py:10-12). The vendor may log the query. Retention, logging and permitted-use terms: see vendor. You must hold your own APILayer account and accept the vendor's terms of service yourself; you must be authorized to query this subject and you remain responsible for each query you send.

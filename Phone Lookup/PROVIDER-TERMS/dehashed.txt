DEHASHED (v2 search API)

Sends a search query containing the subject's phone number, both E.164 and digits-only forms (`phone:"<e164>" OR phone:<digits>`), in a POST body to api.dehashed.com, authenticated with your API key in the `DeHashed-Api-Key` header (providers/dehashed.py:9-13). The vendor may log the query, and results may contain breached personal data. Retention, logging and permitted-use terms: see vendor. You must hold your own DeHashed account and accept the vendor's terms of service yourself; you must be authorized to query this subject and you remain responsible for each query you send.
